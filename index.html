1. Ribcage breathing

Fixes rib flare

2 min

Inhale: ribs widen into your hands.
Exhale: ribs glide down and in.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Hands on lower ribs, knees bent, feet flat.
  • 2Exhale slowly, with a gentle pelvic floor lift.
  • 3Low back stays softly neutral. Aim for 8 to 10 slow breaths.
Watch for ribs poking up toward the ceiling as you inhale.

2. Heel slides

Deep core, pelvic floor

2 min

Start: both knees bent, ribs down.
Slide: exhale and slide one heel out.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Slide only as far as your ribs and low back stay still.
  • 2Inhale to bring the heel back and release.
  • 3Alternate legs. 8 to 10 per side.
Watch for belly doming or your low back arching off the floor.

3. Glute bridge

Pelvic floor, glutes

2 min

Start: feet flat, arms at your sides.
Lift: shoulders, hips, knees in a line.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, press through your feet and roll up.
  • 2Inhale at the top. Relax the pelvic floor as you lower.
  • 38 to 10 slow reps.
Watch for arching your low back or flaring your ribs at the top. Stop a little lower.

4. Bird dog

Deep core, back

2 min

Start: hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
Reach: opposite arm and leg, hips level.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, and reach to the length of your body.
  • 2Hold 2 seconds, then return. Pretend a tray of water sits on your back.
  • 36 to 8 per side.
Watch for your belly sagging or ribs flaring. Shorten the reach if so.

5. Side plank from knees

Side core, ribs over hips

2 min

Start: elbow under shoulder, knees bent.
Lift: shoulder, hip, knee in a line.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, and raise your hips.
  • 2Breathe steadily and hold 20 to 30 seconds.
  • 3Both sides, twice each.
Watch for a shrugged shoulder, hips rolling forward or back, or doming down the belly.

Rowing, easy

After the core routine

10 min

Catch: shins vertical, arms long.
Finish: legs straight, slight lean back.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Drive: legs, then lean, then arms. Recover in reverse. Exhale on the drive.
  • 2Easy pace: you can speak in full sentences. Keep the damper low.
  • 3Start at 10 minutes, about 18 to 22 strokes a minute.
Watch for arching at the finish. That is rib flare. Stop the lean sooner and keep ribs down.

Cat-cow

Spine, hips, shoulders

1 min

Cow: belly softens, chest and tailbone lift.
Cat: round your back, chin toward chest, ribs draw in.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Inhale into cow, exhale into cat. Move slowly.
  • 2Hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  • 3Add a gentle pelvic floor lift as you round.
Watch for wrists or knees hurting. Pad them and use a smaller range. Keep ribs soft in cow.

Child's pose

Low back, hips, shoulders

1 min

Start: on hands and knees.
Fold: hips back toward heels, forehead down, arms long.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Breathe into your back ribs.
  • 2Knees wide if your belly or chest need room.
  • 3Soften your jaw and shoulders.
Watch for knee or hip pressure. Put a pillow between hips and heels.

Low lunge

Hip flexors, front of hips

1 min

Start: back knee down, hands on front thigh.
Sink: hips glide forward, ribs stay stacked over hips.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Pad the back knee. Tuck your tailbone slightly.
  • 2Feel the stretch at the front of the back hip.
  • 3Switch sides at the halfway beep.
Watch for arching your low back. Front knee stays over the ankle.

Knees to chest

Low back, hips

1 min

Start: feet flat, knees bent.
Hug: draw both knees toward your chest, tailbone heavy.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Keep your head and shoulders down on the mat.
  • 2Breathe slowly into your low back.
  • 3Small circles with your knees are fine.
Watch for neck tension. Keep your head down and chin level.

Rag doll fold

Back, hamstrings, neck

1 min

Start: hinge from the hips, hands on shins, knees soft.
Fold: let your head and arms hang heavy.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Bend your knees as much as you like.
  • 2Sway gently side to side. Let your neck go.
  • 3Roll up slowly, head last.
Watch for dizziness. Come up slowly and pause.

Melting heart

Shoulders, upper back

1 min

Start: hands and knees, hips over knees.
Melt: walk hands forward, chest melts toward the floor.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Keep your hips high over your knees.
  • 2Forehead or chin to the mat.
  • 3Breathe into your upper back and shoulders.
Watch for low back arching or ribs flaring. Keep ribs drawn down and stop sinking the chest.

Seated neck release

Neck, upper shoulders

1 min

Start: sit tall, arm resting down.
Tilt: ear toward shoulder, hand rests on head, other hand reaches down.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Let the weight of your hand do the work. Do not pull.
  • 2Breathe slowly and soften your jaw.
  • 3Switch sides at the halfway beep.
Watch for sharp pain or tingling down an arm. Stop.

Seated side bend

Side body, shoulders, ribs

1 min

Start: sit tall on both sit bones.
Bend: reach one arm overhead and lean to the side.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Lengthen up first, then bend.
  • 2Ribs stay stacked. Do not flare them.
  • 3Switch sides at the halfway beep.
Watch for leaning forward or back. Stay in one flat plane.

Rib flare, pelvic floor, calm

Exhale: ribs glide down and in.

Tap the picture to freeze it.

  • 1Hands on lower ribs, knees bent, feet flat.

  • 3Relax your jaw, shoulders and pelvic floor on the inhale.
Watch for ribs poking up toward the ceiling as you inhale.
