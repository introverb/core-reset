    10-minute core reset

    Rib flare, pelvic floor, deep core

    5 moves

    • ●Pelvic floor lift: a gentle lift at about 30% effort, as if stopping a stream of urine. Fully release on the way back.

    Stop if you notice leaking, pelvic heaviness, or a ridge or doming down the middle of your belly.

    1. Ribcage breathing

    Fixes rib flare

    2 min

    Ribs widen into your hands and press toward the floor.Ribs glide down and in, like closing an umbrella.

    • 1Hands on lower ribs, knees bent, feet flat.
    • 2Exhale slowly, with a gentle pelvic floor lift.
    • 3Low back stays softly neutral. Aim for 8 to 10 slow breaths.
    Watch for ribs poking up toward the ceiling as you inhale.

    2. Heel slides

    Deep core, pelvic floor

    2 min

    Both knees bent. Ribs down, belly gently drawn in.Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, and slide one heel out.

    • 1Slide only as far as your ribs and low back stay still.
    • 2Inhale to bring the heel back and release.
    • 3Alternate legs. 8 to 10 per side.
    Watch for belly doming or your low back arching off the floor.

    3. Glute bridge

    Pelvic floor, glutes

    2 min

    Feet flat, hip-width apart, arms resting at your sides.Shoulders, hips and knees line up. Ribs stay down.

    • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, press through your feet and roll up.
    • 2Inhale at the top. Relax the pelvic floor as you lower.
    • 38 to 10 slow reps.
    Watch for arching your low back or flaring your ribs at the top. Stop a little lower.

    4. Bird dog

    Deep core, back

    2 min

    Hands under shoulders, knees under hips. Back flat.Reach opposite arm and leg. Hips stay level.

    • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, and reach to the length of your body.
    • 2Hold 2 seconds, then return. Pretend a tray of water sits on your back.
    • 36 to 8 per side.
    Watch for your belly sagging or ribs flaring. Shorten the reach if so.

    5. Side plank from knees

    Side core, ribs over hips

    2 min

    Elbow under shoulder, knees bent and stacked.Hips up. Shoulder, hip and knee make one line.

    • 1Exhale, lift the pelvic floor, and raise your hips.
    • 2Breathe steadily and hold 20 to 30 seconds.
    • 3Both sides, twice each.
    Watch for a shrugged shoulder, hips rolling forward or back, or doming down the belly.

    Rowing, easy

    After the core routine

    10 min

    Shins vertical, arms long, ribs stacked over hips.Legs straight, lean back slightly, hands to lower ribs.

    • 1Drive: legs, then lean, then arms. Recover in reverse. Exhale on the drive.
    • 2Easy pace: you can speak in full sentences. Keep the damper low.
    • 3Start at 10 minutes, about 18 to 22 strokes a minute.
    Watch for arching at the finish. That is rib flare. Stop the lean sooner and keep ribs down.
